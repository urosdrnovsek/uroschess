# Learner review for opening courses

The automated checks confirm board paths, persistence, navigation, and layout.
A short session with a child and an adult facilitator is still needed to see
whether the explanations make sense. Do not report test completion as proof of
learning.

Start with fresh progress and let the child choose either player in **Learn →
Openings**. Let them use hints if they want. Observe, without directing them:

1. Can they find an animal-coach course and start its first lesson?
2. After a move, can they say why it helped in their own words?
3. Can they try the final, different position before asking for a hint?
4. Can they leave and find the same course and their saved progress again?
5. If opening basics is unfinished, do they understand the Practise basics
   suggestion and find their way back to the player?
6. After finishing, does Watch archival game clearly feel different from the
   practice position, and can they return to the finished lesson?

Ask what “Inspired by [player]'s games” means to them. Check that they do not
think the player wrote the advice or made the exact moves on the synthetic
practice board. Record the words or controls that caused confusion, the text
size and window used, and whether help was needed. Keep feedback anonymous;
the app does not need a child account for this review.
