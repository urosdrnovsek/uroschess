# Chess tips and coach portraits

The app uses two original fictional animal coaches: Bruno the Bear and Olive
the Owl. Each has twelve short, original teaching tips. The cards do not use
quotation marks or suggest that a real person gave the advice.

A random coach appears at launch. Opening another screen, clicking the portrait,
or pressing N picks a different coach. Ordinary redraws and resizes do not
change the coach. The previous card slides and fades into the new one over
0.48 seconds. The card and sketch colours follow the active theme.

The portraits are generated black-ink illustrations converted to 320×320 BMPs
for the pygame build. Package data includes both portraits.
